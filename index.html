GETTING STARTED

How it works

  1. 01

    Choose your settings

    Select your preferred language and currency. The selected currency is used for Target Revenue and Average Order Value.

  2. 02

    Set the campaign period

    Choose the Campaign Start and Campaign End dates. LeadPredictor uses this period to distribute the cumulative pipeline requirements across the campaign months.

  3. 03

    Set your revenue targets

    Enter the Target Revenue (A) and Average Order Value (B).

  4. 04

    Set your conversion rates

    Choose the Lead Conversion Rate (C) and Prospect Conversion Rate (D).

  5. 05

    Calculate your pipeline

    Click Calculate to see how many clients, leads and prospects are required to achieve your revenue target.

Calculation logic

LeadPredictor works backwards from your revenue target to calculate the size of the sales pipeline required to achieve your goal.

Target Revenue (A) ÷ Average Order Value (B)
↓
Required Clients (X) X = A / B
→
Required Clients (X) ÷ Lead Conversion Rate (C)
↓
Required Leads (Y) Y = X / C
→
Required Leads (Y) ÷ Prospect Conversion Rate (D)
↓
Required Prospects (Z) Z = Y / D

The final result tells you how many prospects you need to reach in order to achieve your revenue target, based on your assumed conversion rates.

Your campaign dates determine the number of months in the projection. LeadPredictor uses a progressive campaign ramp-up model, where pipeline acquisition starts more gradually and increases as the campaign develops.

The monthly chart shows cumulative progress towards the final target using a quadratic progression, reaching 100% of the required pipeline by the final month. Bars show cumulative requirements; tooltips also show the new amount required during each month.

Progressive monthly calculation

Cumulative target at month n Cumulative(n) = Total × (n / M)²

n = current campaign month · M = total campaign months · Total = final required clients, leads or prospects

New requirement during month n Monthly(n) = Cumulative(n) − Cumulative(n−1)

For Month 1: Monthly(1) = Cumulative(1)

Example — 400 Required Clients over 3 months

Month 1

400 × (1/3)² ≈ 44 cumulative clients

New clients this month: 44

Month 2

400 × (2/3)² ≈ 178 cumulative clients

New clients this month: 178 − 44 = 134

Month 3

400 × (3/3)² = 400 cumulative clients

New clients this month: 400 − 178 = 222

The same progression is applied to Required Leads (Y) and Required Prospects (Z). This models a campaign that starts gradually and accelerates over time, while reaching 100% of the required pipeline by the final month.
